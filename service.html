Outreach

Bringing more people into computing

I founded the ACM-W chapter at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, mentored high school researchers through the Saturday Academy ASE program and the SWE Merit Certificate program, and have worked with NCWIT, BRAID, and Grace Hopper programs to support women in computing. I have also consulted for the Apache Software Foundation, the Linux Foundation, GitHub, and Globant.
